Imsak: Quran App Blocker

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

The short version. Your reading, your streak and the apps you lock stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't sell anything.

Imsak is made by Frail App Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Imsak app does with information.

Your reading

Your daily goal, reading position, bookmarks, streak, the days you read and your settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. The app never sends the Quran text you read anywhere.

Screen Time

Imsak uses Apple's Screen Time features, with your permission, only to lock the apps you choose until you read. Apple gives Imsak your choices as private tokens, so Imsak cannot see the names of your apps or what you do in them. These tokens are saved only on your iPhone and are never uploaded or shared. Imsak does not monitor anyone else. You can turn this off any time in iOS Settings.

Location

If you turn on prayer lock, Imsak asks for your location once to work out prayer times. The location is rounded, saved only on your iPhone and never sent anywhere. Prayer times are calculated on your iPhone. You can turn this off any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services.

Purchases

Imsak Plus purchases are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your Apple ID has Imsak Plus. RevenueCat receives purchase information and a random app user ID, not your name, email or reading. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Quran text updates

Imsak keeps the Quran text up to date with Quran Foundation's Content Sync. About once a day, the app asks a content server run by us (on Cloudflare) whether a new version of the text is available and downloads it if so. The request has no name, account, device ID, reading history or other information about you. Like any server, it sees your IP address when your phone connects, and we do not store it or use it to identify you. If your phone is offline, the app keeps working with the text it already has.

Notifications

If you allow them, Imsak sends reminders from your phone, for example about your next page or the end of a free trial. These are created on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no ad networks.
  • No analytics and no tracking across other companies' apps and websites.
  • No selling or renting of information.
  • No accounts, emails or phone numbers collected.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything Imsak stores is on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

Imsak is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and for big changes we'll tell you in the app.
