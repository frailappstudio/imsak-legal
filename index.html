Support

Imsak holds back the apps you choose until you read your next page of the Quran.

Need help? Email frailappstudio@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We read every message.

Common questions

My app isn't locking. Check that Screen Time access is on for Imsak in iOS Settings > Screen Time > Apps with Screen Time Access. Then open Imsak and check the app you picked is still selected.

How do I change the locked app? Open Imsak, go to Settings > Blocking, and pick a different app.

How do I cancel Imsak Plus? iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Imsak. Cancelling keeps Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

How does billing work? Imsak Plus is billed through your Apple ID. Payment is charged at confirmation, or after the free trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Lifetime is a one-time purchase.

I reinstalled and Plus is gone. Open Settings in Imsak and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID.
